ℹ️ About MoneyLens

Your personal budget tracker for smarter financial decisions

🎯

Our Mission

MoneyLens was created to help individuals take control of their personal finances through simple, intuitive, and real-time budget tracking. We believe that everyone deserves clarity about their spending habits and the tools to make better financial decisions.

⚡

Key Features

  • ✓ Real-time balance updates
  • ✓ Income and expense tracking
  • ✓ Category-based spending analysis
  • ✓ Interactive expense charts
  • ✓ Transaction history with filters
  • ✓ Automatic data persistence (localStorage)
📖

How to Use

  1. Add Transactions: Use the form on the Dashboard to add income or expenses.
  2. View Insights: Check the expense chart and category breakdown.
  3. Manage History: Visit the Transactions page to filter and delete entries.
  4. Track Progress: Your balance updates automatically with every change.
💡

Financial Tips

  • 📌 Follow the 50/30/20 rule (Needs/Wants/Savings)
  • 📌 Track every expense, no matter how small
  • 📌 Review your budget weekly
  • 📌 Set realistic savings goals
